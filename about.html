About Rozana

Rozana is a free daily paper for students preparing for SSC, banking, railway and UPSC exams. Every day at midnight IST a new paper goes live: one word puzzle, five General Studies questions with SSC-style marking, and one reasoning question.

Questions are written from primary sources such as PIB releases, the Constitution of India, RBI publications and standard references. Every question is reviewed before it is published.
